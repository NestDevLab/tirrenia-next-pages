The story

One peninsula. A thousand coasts. One Mediterranean soul.

From Campania to Sicily, from Tuscany to Puglia, Tirrenia tells the story of Italy as it looks towards the sea, from its great islands to the cities of the peninsula.

It begins with a passion rooted in Amalfi and Naples: espresso at the counter, warm hospitality, and full flavours. From there, the journey opens to the Mediterranean through pastries, wines, dishes, and products inspired by its coasts.

The next version

Less to do. More done well.

The relaunch does not need an endless menu or more people behind the counter. It needs clear choices, products worth their price, and someone who knows how to tell their story.

01

Prepare better

A tighter menu, batch preparation where quality allows, and generous dishes that earn their price.

02

Sell with purpose

Explain the product, offer a good alternative, and suggest a pairing or dessert when it genuinely fits.

03

Open when it matters

Focus hours and people on the moments that create value, without creating needless work.

This is an operating direction to test and measure, not a guarantee of sales or margin.
