This notice covers the Club visit, subrent and business-sale enquiry forms on Tirrenia Next. Last updated 17 September 2026.

Who handles your details

Tirrenia V.O.F., Kruisweg 42, 2011 LD Haarlem, Netherlands, is responsible for these requests. For questions or requests about your data, contact us at the shop or by phone/WhatsApp at +31 6 4416 4894.

What we collect and why

We collect your name, email address or phone number, the preferred visit date, duration and number of seats if relevant, and any optional details you send. We use this information only to assess and answer your request, arrange a visit if we agree, and keep a record of its status. This is based on taking steps at your request before a possible agreement. Sending a form does not reserve a seat or create a purchase.

Our hosting provider processes the form data in a private Cloudflare D1 database with European Union jurisdiction. The website is served through GitHub Pages and Cloudflare. We do not use these form details for marketing or sell them to others. Please do not put medical or other sensitive information in the optional message.

How long we keep it

Club requests are deleted after 90 days and subrent or business-sale enquiries after 180 days, measured from submission. A technical rate-limit record, derived from your IP address with a secret hash, is deleted after 48 hours. If a request becomes part of a separate transaction or legal matter, the relevant records may need a different retention period; we will explain that separately.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to or restrict its use. Contact us using the details above. We may need to verify your identity before providing data. You can also complain to the Dutch Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
