Kruisweg 42 · Haarlem

A place for your next chapter.

Tirrenia is looking for an established food business to continue the Italian gastronomy at Kruisweg 42, Haarlem, under a sublease. Tell us about your project and ask for a visit.

Tell us what you have in mind. This is an enquiry; it creates no booking or agreement. We will contact you personally about availability and conditions.

What is on offer

A working production kitchen with a shop front in the centre of Haarlem: convection oven, four-burner range, two-chamber pizza oven and generous refrigeration. There is no fryer and no blast chiller. It suits batch cooking, regeneration, catering preparation or a pickup point for an existing business.

The premises are designated for Italian deli and traiteur use, with limited seated service. A concept that moves away from Italian food is not possible here.

Who we are looking for

Operators who are already running a food business between Haarlem and Amsterdam, such as an Italian deli, a caterer or a pizza and focaccia producer, and who want a second kitchen, a production base or a retail point without building one from scratch.

How it works

  • The head lease stays with Tirrenia V.O.F. A sublease needs the landlord's written consent and is agreed case by case.
  • You pay a monthly fee for the space, agreed personally. Utilities are advanced by Tirrenia and reimbursed for your share.
  • The Tirrenia name, website, digital platform and supplier network are separate, optional services. You can run under your own brand.

Nothing on this page is an offer. Conditions are discussed personally and confirmed in writing.

What happens next

  1. We read your enquiry and reply personally, usually within a few days.
  2. If it looks like a fit, we arrange a visit to Kruisweg 42 by appointment.
  3. Scope and written terms are discussed after the visit. Nothing is agreed before a signed document.
